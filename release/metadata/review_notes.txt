Islora is a modular island-building game. No account, login or demo credentials are required. Gameplay and saves work locally on the device.

1. On first launch, tap Claim to close the Daily Bonus overlay.
2. Tap the + on the first unlocked plot. A new player starts with Farm available, so construction opens directly.
3. Drag a Foundation from its icon in the bottom tray onto the bottom row of the grid.
4. Drag a Field above the Foundation. Workers complete construction over time; built fields produce Food.
5. Tap Done to return to the island view. Tap an existing building to edit it.
6. Open Contracts and claim the First Harvest Journey reward after the Field finishes. The Journey has 19 goals. The rotating board has three City Orders; accept one and Deliver when its required stock is available. A lone Field produces 18 Food/minute before coastal/event bonuses, so the first 100-Food shipment takes under five minutes after construction.
7. Build a Solar Panel and claim the next Journey goal to reach Level 2 and unlock Processing. Power Plants unlock at Level 3, Data Centers at Level 4, advanced modules at Levels 5–8.
8. Codex contains synergy discoveries and saved blueprints. Profile shows workers, resources and experience. Project shows local Skybridge contributions. Settings contains the website link, mute control and Reset Game confirmation.

The World Project currently tracks this device's contribution only; it is not shared online. The game interface is in English. No in-app purchases or advertising SDKs are included in this build.
