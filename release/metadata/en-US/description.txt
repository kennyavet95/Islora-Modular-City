Build a little island, one module at a time.

In Islora, you design the buildings yourself. Place foundations, connect modules and find a layout that balances production with structural support.

MAKE EVERY MODULE COUNT
Develop farms, power plants and data centers as you level up.
Drag modules onto your building grid. Combine fields, greenhouses, water tanks, offices and solar panels to give each construction a purpose.

BUILD WITH SUPPORT IN MIND
Watch the load on your structure. Reinforce stressed modules and rethink unsupported sections before they collapse.

DISCOVER BETTER COMBINATIONS
Place compatible modules next to each other to discover synergies and improve your output. Keep track of discoveries in the Codex.

FOLLOW YOUR ISLAND JOURNEY
Complete 19 building and delivery goals, choose from a rotating board of city orders, and take advantage of short production events. Unlock Hydroponic Gardens, Coastal Turbines, Research Servers and Island Depots through Level 8.

SAVE YOUR IDEAS
Save a building as a blueprint and reuse its layout on another suitable plot. Construction still uses your resources and workers.

GROW AT YOUR OWN PACE
Unlock additional plots, collect daily rewards and return to resources produced while you were away, for up to eight hours.

Play without an account. Your city and progress are saved on your device. The interface is in English.
