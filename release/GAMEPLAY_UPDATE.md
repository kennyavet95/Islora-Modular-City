# Gameplay and presentation update — 20 October 2026

- Three simultaneous City Orders with 13 delivery patterns: food, industrial and mixed shipments. Industries open by level, food orders remain available, and unaccepted orders can be replaced.
- 19 persistent, once-only Journey rewards covering production, expansion, synergies, blueprints, deliveries and the Skybridge. Current goal appears on the island.
- Four later modules: Hydroponic Garden (5), Coastal Turbine (6), Research Server (7), Island Depot (8). They reuse the game's existing art and structural/synergy rules.
- Production balanced in units per minute; a single built Field can supply the first 100-Food shipment in under five minutes before event bonuses. A normal-time rules test verifies this; a full real-time campaign balance playtest is still pending.
- Short production events, locked-in high-demand coin payouts, visible order/resource progress, income and next unlocks in Profile.
- Construction workers with tools and dust, module completion bursts, operating lights, animated truck wheels, water glints, birds and reward celebrations. New ambient/celebration motion respects Reduce Motion.
- Nine original synthesized WAV cues. Mute persists between launches.
- Explicit sheet Close buttons, removal confirmation, clearer first-run instructions and full module costs. Reset uses the current session's save store. Removing/collapsing modules releases assigned workers.
- Input-constrained processing: no Goods without Food. Offline production now uses the same recipes, district bonuses and event boundaries, with one-minute steps and the eight-hour limit.
- Legacy saves retain cities and accepted orders. New Journey IDs and delivery statistics are optional; new order counts begin with this update, without inventing previous statistics.

No multiplayer, online economy, advertising or IAP has been added. Publishing and signing still require the new account. Apple approval and player retention cannot be established by automated tests.
